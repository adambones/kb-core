# The decision

Answer the ask with a decision: the option you would take, why, what it costs,
and each alternative you rejected with the reason it lost. Answer every part
the brief says the answer must settle, and where the source cannot settle one,
say what would.

A cost is the work an option takes and the risk it carries: the changes,
reviews and decisions it needs, and the dates outside the work it must meet.
Estimate no time the user's own pace decides, and fix a date only where
something outside the work fixes it.

End with the steps that carry the decision out, in the order their
dependencies force, each with what shows it done, so that a session that has
read nothing but the record can start. Where a step turns on a precise form
that prose would leave ambiguous, show that form.
