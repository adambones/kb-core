# Messaging

A message about the work is a pointer to where its substance lives: the fewest words that let the reader act, then a link to the home. These rules govern every such message and never a home, whose depth its own rubric licenses, because simplifying a home is damage rather than discipline. Each rule names something checkable against the artifact rather than against the writer's memory, and one keyword names the whole rule, so correcting a draft costs a word. Each knowledge base names who its messages reach among its bindings.

## What a message carries

**One Home** (`RUBRIC.md`) governs a message as it governs a document.

**Projection.** Name what the change means for the reader, never its contents. Whoever wants the inventory opens the artifact. The deletion rules compress a sentence once it has the projection, and never generate one.

**Identifier Ban.** A message never uses the source's own identifiers, because the artifact it points to carries them.

## Shape

**Shape Follows Cardinality.** Prose is the default. One point is a short paragraph, never a bullet. A flat list earns its place only on parallel, independent items the reader scans rather than reads.

**One Claim.** A sentence makes one claim. When a title or an ask has two to carry, write the one claim that covers both, or keep the more important and drop the other. Joining them is not a third option, whatever does the joining.

**Named Things.** Use concrete nouns and active verbs. Say what does what to what.

## Deletion rules

Read each sentence and ask whether removing it changes what the reader can do. If not, cut it. The rules are scissors, not sandpaper: leave the fragment a cut makes rather than smoothing it back into a sentence.

**Plain Word.** A shorter, simpler sentence with the same meaning is sitting unused.

**State it once.** A sentence restating the previous fact as its complement adds words, not information.

**Ghost Correction** and **No Examples** (`RUBRIC.md`) govern a message as they govern a document. A list of the instances a statement covers dies from its first item.
